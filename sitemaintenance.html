🔐 Google Sign-In

Allow staff and members to sign in with their Google accounts. You need a Google Client ID from Google Cloud Console — create OAuth 2.0 credentials and add your site URL as an authorised JavaScript origin.

ℹ️ How it works

Once configured:

  • Staff can sign into the admin panel with their Google account (set their Google email in the Team tab)
  • Members can sign into the members portal with their Google account (set their Google email in the Members tab)
  • Full Access staff automatically get member portal access when logged in

This line goes above the sender's name on every email sent from the site — by any team member, and on automatic emails. Leave it blank for no sign-off.

Contact Us alerts

🔔 Where Contact Us alerts go

When someone fills in the Contact Us form, an alert is sent here and they get an automatic "we've received your message" reply. Leave blank to turn the alerts off.

Email addresses

📧 Addresses

Emails sent to any of these addresses arrive in the dashboard Inbox, and staff you allow can send from them. Tick No-reply for an address nobody checks (e.g. noreply@) — every email from it says "Please do not reply — this mailbox is not monitored", and the Contact Us automatic reply is sent from it.

How to get the token (one-off, about 15 minutes)
  1. In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account (Business or Creator — it's free).
  2. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps, log in with Facebook, and click Create app. Choose the Instagram use case (managing messages and content on Instagram).
  3. In the app, open Instagram → API setup with Instagram business login.
  4. Under Generate access tokens, click Add account, log in to the Qur'an Detectives Instagram and allow access.
  5. Click Generate token next to the account, copy it, and paste it above.
